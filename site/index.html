Exploitation ReportNovember 26, 2026

Executive Summary

Multiple critical vulnerabilities in enterprise and infrastructure products have been added to CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog or are under active exploitation assessment. GeoServer flaws including CVE-2026-120705 and CVE-2026-129243 are associated with observed in-the-wild probe activity against honeypot networks, while Drupal CVE-2026-132165 (Fail2Install) carries a potential exploitation status with confirmed patch availability. A critical unpatched buffer overflow in Danfoss AK-SM 8xxA Series controllers (CVE-2026-129713) has been linked to attacks on supermarkets and food retailers across Europe, with no fix currently available.

Several Mitel MiCollab vulnerabilities, including command injection CVE-2026-133323 rated CVSS 10.0, and Vates Xen Orchestra CVE-2026-133469 are also listed in the KEV catalog with exploitation evidence reported by vendors and threat intelligence firms. The PHP2Shell chain (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619) and Fragnesia (CVE-2026-46300) have been observed chained against Palo Alto Networks and Check Point devices with exposed management interfaces. Separately, North Korean-linked PolinRider activity continues to poison GitHub repositories and npm packages, and malicious NuGet packages have targeted .NET developers through fake job assessments, though these campaign items do not map to specific CVE identifiers in the supplied sources.

Attack Vectors and Techniques

  • Unauthenticated Web Feature Service Request: Exploiting CVE-2026-120705 via a single unauthenticated WFS request to read local files or fetch adversary-selected URLs
  • Service Security Rules Bypass: Using prefixed service names such as x/WFS to bypass admin-only rules for stored queries (CVE-2026-129243)
  • Stored Query Probing: Creating and invoking stored queries to validate vulnerability, then deleting them immediately afterward (observed against CVE-2026-120705 and CVE-2026-129244)
  • Remember-Me Cookie Forgery: Using a predictable hard-coded signing key to forge administrator authentication cookies (CVE-2026-129245)
  • DLL Sideloading: Staging a DLL sideloading pair around Microsoft On-Screen Keyboard (osk.exe) to execute attacker-controlled code
  • Browser Management Policy Abuse: Forcing Chrome, Edge, and Firefox to install malicious extensions via enterprise management tools and MDM enrollment
  • Adversary-in-the-Middle Phishing: DOM mirroring technique where the attacker's browser relays a genuine sign-in page to the victim (DOMDIFFER kit)
  • Supply Chain Poisoning: Hijacking maintainer accounts to push malicious packages and force-push malicious commits to open-source repositories (PolinRider campaign)

Threat Actor Activities

  • North Korean State-Sponsored Actors (PolinRider/Contagious Interview): PolinRider is assessed as a sub-cluster operating in parallel with the long-running Contagious Interview campaign; actors use fraudulent recruitment campaigns, hijacked maintainer accounts, and compromised developer machines to poison GitHub repositories and npm packages targeting corporate environments; activity dates back to at least February 2025 based on blockchain records
  • Unknown Threat Actor (Mitel MiCollab Campaign): An unknown threat actor may have been weaponizing MiCollab flaws as zero-days as part of a suspected AI-assisted data theft campaign; deployment of a web shell was observed against one victim organization a day before Mitel released patches; the actor harvested credentials and stole sensitive business data from a line-of-business database server within hours
  • Iranian Threat Group (Shahid Kaveh / Cyber Aveng3rs / Cyber Isnaad Front): France attributed October 2026 attacks on French supermarket sites and November 2026 attacks across Belgium, the Netherlands, Sweden, Luxembourg, Ireland, Romania, and Estonia to this IRGC-linked unit; attackers compromised Danfoss AK-SM/AK-SC and Copeland/Dixell XWEB refrigeration controllers and remotely altered defrost settings or shut down cooling systems
  • Volant Manticore: A previously undocumented Iranian threat group targeting Cypriot organizations as part of an intelligence-gathering effort for Tehran
  • SYS010 Operator: Delivers a new version of the SYS01 infostealer through Meta ads impersonating Wallpaper Engine and Lively Wallpapers; targets Facebook and Meta business accounts to steal Gmail and Outlook emails and session cookies
  • Remus Operators: Marketed an alleged Gmail zero-day that was actually a malicious Chrome extension; steals unread emails from signed-in Gmail accounts and survives password changes and session revocations
  • GoodGuys Phishing Operators: Impersonate major brands including Google, Nvidia, Accenture, and SpaceX using fake paid expert interview invitations to steal corporate Google and Okta credentials; use legitimate email platforms including HireVue, SendGrid, Mandrill, and Cvent
  • Multiple IP Addresses (GeoServer Honeypot Activity): Threat actors from multiple IP addresses were observed validating vulnerable GeoServer systems by creating stored queries, checking whether they were created, and deleting them immediately afterward; associated with CVE-2026-120705 and CVE-2026-129244 probing
